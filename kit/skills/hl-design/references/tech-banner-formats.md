## Layout Rules

- Keep critical content inside the central `70-80%` safe zone.
- For YouTube channel art, keep all essential content inside the `1546 x 423 px` safe area.
- Use one CTA per banner; keep it visually dominant and mobile-tappable.
- Start CTA copy with an action verb such as `Get`, `Start`, `Download`, or `Claim`.
- CTA height for touch targets: at least `44px`.
- Prefer CTA placement near the terminal scan area, usually bottom-right unless composition needs a different exit point.

## Type And Text Density

- Max `2` typefaces per banner.
- Digital minimums: body `16px+`, headline `32px+`.
- Keep contrast at or above WCAG AA `4.5:1`.
- Aim for `<= 7` words per line and `<= 3` text lines in tighter ad units.
- Meta-style paid ads should stay under roughly `20%` text coverage.
- Social cover compositions often work best around a `60/40` image-to-text balance.

## Print Specs

- `300 DPI` minimum; `150 DPI` acceptable for large-format distant viewing.
- Add `3-5 mm` bleed on all sides.
- Prepare print exports in `CMYK`.
- For distant viewing, start headlines around `70 pt+` and scale with viewing distance.
- Calibrate distant-view headlines at roughly `1 pt per foot` of viewing distance.
