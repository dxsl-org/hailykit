# Article Playbook

Use for news, blog posts, and op-eds. All default to short-form. A multi-part investigative series treats each part as a unit and re-evaluates the track when the whole crosses the long-form threshold.

Press releases route to `playbook-marketing-copy.md`. Delivered persuasive arguments route to `playbook-speech.md`; published advocacy remains an op-ed.

## News Article

**Skeleton:** lede (5W1H, at most 30 words) → nut graf → facts in descending importance → attributed quotes → optional context.

**Evidence:** named sources, verifiable quotes, dated facts.

**Publication integrity:** append new facts with an explicit `Updated:` timestamp. Correct false published facts with a visible correction note; never silently overwrite attributed history.

**Review:** lede covers 5W1H; ordering is inverted-pyramid rather than chronological; every quote/source is attributable; headline matches the body's actual claim.

**Length/unit:** straight news usually 400–800 words; feature length follows the brief. Close as one unit or split opening from later fact blocks.

## Blog Post

**Skeleton:** hook → thesis/promise → H2 sections matching search intent → conclusion/CTA.

**Evidence:** named sources for factual claims, verifiable statistics, and dated facts. Confirm search intent during Recon; each H2 answers a distinct reader query.

**Review:** payoff appears in the first 1–2 sentences; sections fulfill declared intent; sources are attributable; CTA and title match the delivered content.

**Maintenance:** an evergreen refresh keeps the URL/slug, records `Updated:`, and becomes a new unit unless the thesis changes enough to require a rewrite.

**Length/unit:** use 1,500–2,500 words as a brief-adjustable starting range; one Build unit per H2.

## Op-Ed

**Skeleton:** hook/setup → thesis by paragraph 2–4 → one sustained evidence-backed argument → strongest opposing argument/concession → actionable close.

**Evidence:** statistics trace to primary data. Address the strongest real objection; do not strawman, cherry-pick, or attack people.

**Review:** one thesis remains throughout; concession is genuine; claims use source data; critique targets arguments; close advances action rather than restating the thesis.

**Length/unit:** default to one unit and about 600–800 words unless the target outlet specifies otherwise.

## Attribution Guardrails

> **Required — attribution-before-print:** no quote, statistic, or named source enters the draft without a matching `research/` entry. Source or hedge unsupported claims; never invent them.

> **Required — op-ed-advocacy-bounded:** advocacy changes stance and structure, never the attribution floor. Persuasion is not permission to fabricate a quote, statistic, source, or opposing position.
