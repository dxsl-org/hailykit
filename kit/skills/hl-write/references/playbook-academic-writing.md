# Academic Writing Playbook

Use for Western essays, Vietnamese tiểu luận, and IMRaD papers. A Vietnamese request defaults to Tiểu Luận; English or unspecified defaults to Western Essay. The approved brief may override the locale default, but structure never changes silently after outline approval.

Record `citation_style` during Recon and apply `references/citation-styles.md`; do not restate style rules here. Single-work close reading routes to `playbook-literary-criticism.md`; theses/dissertations route to `playbook-academic-thesis.md`.

Western essays and IMRaD papers default to short-form. Tiểu Luận defaults to long-form because its usual multi-chapter structure crosses the threshold.

## Western Academic Essay

**Skeleton:** introduction with debatable thesis → body paragraphs (topic sentence, evidence, analysis; one claim each) → synthesis conclusion with no new claims.

**Evidence:** every factual claim is cited and the argument's premises are grounded in literature.

**Review:** thesis is specific/debatable; each paragraph connects to it; citations follow the chosen style; conclusion adds no claim.

**Length/unit:** normally 1,500–3,000 words; one Build unit per introduction, body-argument group, or conclusion.

## Tiểu Luận

**Skeleton:** Mở bài (đề tài, lý do, phạm vi, mục đích) → Thân bài (cơ sở lý luận → thực trạng → đánh giá/giải pháp) → Kết luận (tổng kết, hạn chế, hướng nghiên cứu) → Tài liệu tham khảo.

> **Required — references-mandatory:** `Tài liệu tham khảo` is always present, formatted, complete, and backed by real sources—even in a shorter tiểu luận.

**Evidence/review:** establish cited cơ sở lý luận before thực trạng; trace every factual assessment to a source; keep the section order; include limitations and future research.

**Length/unit:** typically about 5,000–10,000 words including front matter; use the long-form track. One Build unit per chương, with Mở bài and Kết luận as units.

## Academic Or Scientific Paper (IMRaD)

**Document order:** Abstract + Keywords → Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → References.

**Build order:** Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Abstract + Keywords.

> **Required — abstract-draft-last:** draft the Abstract last from completed IMRaD sections, then assemble it first. Keep it citation-free and limited to claims established in the body. Verify it exists in both outline and assembled manuscript.

Default Abstract: 200–250 words; Keywords: 3–6 terms. The brief or target venue overrides both.

**Evidence:** real literature establishes the gap; Methods is reproducible; every claim maps to a retrievable citation.

**Review:** Results reports findings without interpretation; Discussion interprets and compares; limitations are specific; citations are verified; Abstract introduces nothing new.

**Length/unit:** venue-specific, often 3,000–8,000 words. One unit per IMRaD section plus the final Abstract/Keywords unit.

## Citation Guardrail

> **Required — verify-before-cite:** verify every citation against a real, retrievable source before Ship. No AI-suggested or secondary citation reaches the manuscript merely because it looks plausible.
